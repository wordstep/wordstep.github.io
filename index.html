Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WordStep: Learn Languages (“WordStep”, “the app”) handles information when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

WordStep is designed to keep your profile information and learning progress on your device. Information such as the name you enter, your selected language, and your learning progress is stored locally and is not sent to the app developer's servers.

Microphone and speech recognition

WordStep may request microphone access when you choose to practise pronunciation. Audio and speech recognition may be processed by Apple's operating-system speech services, depending on your device settings and the service available. WordStep does not send recordings to the app developer's own server. Review Apple's privacy information and your device settings for details about system speech services.

Subscriptions and payments

WordStep may offer an auto-renewable subscription, such as WordStep Premium. Purchases and payment processing are handled by Apple through the App Store. The app developer does not receive your full payment-card details. Apple may provide limited transaction and subscription status information needed to manage purchases.

Data collection and sharing

The app developer does not operate a server that collects the profile information or learning progress described above. WordStep does not sell personal information. This statement does not cover information Apple or operating-system services process independently when you use App Store purchases or speech features.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored profile information and learning progress can generally be removed by deleting the app or using relevant in-app controls. Subscription management and cancellation are handled through your Apple Account settings.

Children's privacy

WordStep is a language-learning app. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children on its own servers. Parents or guardians can contact the developer with privacy questions.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this page, with the updated date shown above.
